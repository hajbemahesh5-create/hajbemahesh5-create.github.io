Privacy Policy for Sketch Flow

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Sketch Flow ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Sketch Flow is a locally-driven sketching app. We do not host, store, or view your personal creative artwork or sketches on external databases or cloud servers. All drawings are processed directly on your mobile device.

However, to help support and maintain the free features of the app via advertisements, we integrate third-party tools that collect technical identifiers.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and use certain non-sensitive data points from your device to optimize and personalize ad tracking performance. This data may include:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (such as Google GAID or Apple IDFA).
  • IP address and general coarse location details.
  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and system analytics performance details.

For more details on how Google uses your data, please read the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal data, please contact us immediately so we can take necessary actions.

4. Security

We are committed to protecting any minimal technical info collected. We use commercially reasonable administrative methods to guard this information, though no wireless network or electronic storage system is completely flawless.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We recommend reviewing this document periodically for any structural changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy framework, you can contact the developer by email at: [sketchflow.support@gmail.com]
